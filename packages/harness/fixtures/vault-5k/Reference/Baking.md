# Baking

BAKING, the action of the verb "to [[Bake|bake]]," a word, in various forms, common
to Teutonic languages (cf. Ger. _backen_), meaning to [[Cook|cook]] by dry heat.
"Baking" is thus primarily applied to [v.03 p.0230] the process of
preparing bread, and is also applied to the hardening by heat or "firing"
of pottery, earthenware or [[Brick|bricks]]. (See BREAD; CERAMICS and BRICK.)
