# Lacunar

LACUNAR, the Latin name in architecture for a panelled or coffered
[[Ceiling|ceiling]] or soffit. The word is derived from _lacuna_, a cavity or
hollow, a [[Blank|blank]], [[Hiatus|hiatus]] or [[Gap|gap]]. The panels or [[Coffer|coffers]] of a ceiling are
by Vitruvius called _lacunaria_.
