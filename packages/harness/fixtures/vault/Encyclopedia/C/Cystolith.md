# Cystolith

CYSTOLITH (Gr. [Greek: kystis], cavity, and [Greek: lithos], stone), a
botanical term for the inorganic [[Concretion|concretions]], usually of calcium
carbonate, formed in a [[Cellulose|cellulose]] [[Matrix|matrix]] in special cells, generally in
the [[Leaf|leaf]] of plants of certain families, e.g. _Ficus elastica_, the
india-rubber plant.
