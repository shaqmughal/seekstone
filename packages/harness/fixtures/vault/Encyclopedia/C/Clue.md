# Clue

CLUE, or CLEW (O. Eng. _cluwe_), originally a [[Ball|ball]] of thread or wool,
the thread of life, which, according to the fable, the [[Fate|Fates]] spin for
every man. The ordinary figurative meaning, a piece of evidence leading
to discovery, or a sign pointing to the right track, is derived from the
story of Theseus, who was guided through the [[Labyrinth|labyrinth]] by the ball of
thread held by Ariadne.
